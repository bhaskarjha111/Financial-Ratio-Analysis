# Financial Ratio Analysis — Apple vs Microsoft vs Samsung

## 📊 Project Overview

This project performs a comparative financial analysis of **Apple, Microsoft, and Samsung Electronics** using financial statement data from their annual reports.

The objective is to evaluate the three companies across key financial dimensions including **valuation, profitability, financial leverage, and shareholder returns**.

Rather than simply calculating financial ratios, the project focuses on interpreting what the ratios reveal about each company's financial position and business performance.

The analysis is implemented using **Python and Excel**, with a structured financial model, ratio calculations, visual dashboards, and analyst-style business insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

The project was developed to:

- Analyze real-world financial statements.
- Calculate and compare key financial ratios.
- Evaluate company profitability and shareholder returns.
- Analyze financial leverage and capital structure.
- Compare market valuation using the P/E ratio.
- Build a structured financial analysis model.
- Visualize company performance through charts and dashboards.
- Convert financial data into meaningful business insights.

---

## 🏢 Companies Analyzed

| Company | Industry | Primary Business Exposure |
|---|---|---|
| Apple | Technology & Consumer Electronics | iPhone, Mac, Services, Wearables |
| Microsoft | Technology & Cloud Computing | Azure, Software, Cloud, Enterprise |
| Samsung Electronics | Technology & Semiconductors | Semiconductors, Smartphones, Electronics |

The companies were selected because they are global technology leaders with significantly different business models, capital structures, and sources of profitability.

This makes the comparison more meaningful than comparing companies operating in exactly the same niche.
